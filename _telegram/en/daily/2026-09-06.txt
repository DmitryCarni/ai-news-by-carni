Day in review for September 6, 2026

The biggest shift: AI is starting to accelerate the creation of the next generation of AI.

OpenAI says its research organization now logs about 3.1 agent workdays for every human workday. The company believes it has reached the “automated research intern” stage: under human direction, an agent can complete well-scoped tasks that would take a skilled researcher several days.

At almost the same time, OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki highlighted the other side of that acceleration: chain-of-thought monitoring is becoming less dependable as models grow more autonomous. The more work agents do on their own, the more important external controls become — permissions, logs, isolation, verification and shutdown mechanisms.

That creates a clear market split: agent productivity is becoming economically measurable, while control of agent actions is turning into infrastructure of its own.

Where product potential is emerging:
— autonomy-readiness audits and secure permission/approval design for business workflows;
— independent logging, verification and policy layers between agents and ERP, CRM or finance systems.
