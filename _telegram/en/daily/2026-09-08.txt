Day in review — September 8, 2026

AI is becoming a system of workers, not a single assistant.

• OpenAI disclosed a research setup involving roughly 10,000 concurrent agents working for about 88 hours on a Navier–Stokes result, followed by Lean formalization and verification with GPT-6 Astra. The mathematics still needs independent acceptance, but the operating model is already notable: AI as a coordinated research organization rather than one model answering one prompt.

• Meta launched Muse, a personal agent available through its app and WhatsApp. It can browse, fill forms, keep working after the app closes, remember long-term context and make purchases, while a separate Sentinel agent reviews outbound actions.

• Mistral raised €3 billion at a valuation of roughly €21 billion. Sovereign AI is becoming a serious infrastructure business spanning models, compute, deployment and data control.

• GPT-6 Astra became generally available on Amazon Bedrock, pushing frontier models deeper into the IAM, logging, networking and procurement systems enterprises already use.

• In finance, Focal AI moved wealth-management agents beyond meeting notes into forms, CRM updates and financial-planning workflows. Aave launched an official MCP server that can build transactions but deliberately cannot sign them or move funds.

Where product opportunities are emerging:
1. Controlled execution agents on top of CRM/ERP: read → prepare → show → execute only after approval.
2. Multi-agent control infrastructure: cost tracking, traces, testing, replay, anomaly detection and policy gates.
