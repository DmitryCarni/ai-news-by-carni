AI NEWS — SEPTEMBER 15

Enterprise AI is becoming specialized: companies are starting to encode their own domain expertise into models while rolling agents out to tens of thousands of employees.

Salesforce and NVIDIA introduced Koa, a CRM reasoning model built by post-training Nemotron 3 Super on synthetic scenarios modeled on nearly three decades of Salesforce CRM workflows. Salesforce controls the weights and runs inference inside its own infrastructure.

Adecco is taking the deployment side to a different scale: Agentforce Coworker is rolling out across 40+ countries to 27,000 employees. The system connects company data, organizational knowledge and specialized recruiting and sales agents, drawing on context from more than 2.5 million agent-candidate interactions.

In finance, Workiva launched Agent Studio, giving finance, audit and compliance teams a no-code way to build governed agents inside regulated workflows.

And Europe is expanding the inference hardware market. Axelera launched its Europa accelerator, announced AI-factory contracts and Dell/Supermicro integrations, and says it is pursuing a potential sales pipeline of up to $1.5 billion — not booked orders or revenue.

The bigger shift: enterprise value is moving beyond the smartest general model toward domain expertise, proprietary context, tools and controlled execution.
