AI News — September 17, 2026

The biggest shift today: AI is already taking a measurable role in building its own next generation.

Anthropic published unusually concrete internal numbers. Claude now “leads” 26% of the company’s AI R&D work under human supervision, while more than 90% of research work involves AI at least as a collaborator. Around 30,000 agents were active at any one time on Anthropic’s main internal research and engineering platform. Every action is screened before execution; across more than a billion decisions in August, roughly one in 47,000 was blocked.

Google is pushing the agent model in another direction. CC is becoming a shared household agent with its own verified Google Account, up to six members, per-person permissions, shared memory, Calendar and Tasks. It can fill forms and manage logistics, but needs permission to act or share information outside the group.

The infrastructure race is also moving below the GPU layer. GlobalFoundries and Marvell are expanding SiGe manufacturing capacity for high-speed optical connectivity in AI data centers. As clusters get larger, moving data between accelerators becomes a performance and energy bottleneck of its own.

In finance, Byzfunder launched TraceDataIQ for small-business underwriting. The company says the platform is built on more than $2 billion of financing history across 35,000+ businesses and combines bank data with a broader financial and operational profile.

Where product opportunity is emerging:
• an agent control plane for identity, permissions, action logs, automated blocking and human approval;
• shared team agents with persistent memory and per-member access controls.
