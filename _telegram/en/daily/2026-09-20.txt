AI day in review — September 20, 2026

China is narrowing another gap in the compute stack.

• CXMT moved its G5 DRAM platform into mass production. The company reports an 11.95 nm active-area half-pitch and at least 50% more gross dies per wafer than its previous generation.

• Two new 24-gigabit LPDDR5X chips are already in volume production, with 50% more capacity than comparable previous-generation CXMT products.

• The US and China opened talks where AI safety sits alongside trade and critical minerals. The agenda includes AI guardrails, self-improving systems and dangerous dual-use applications.

On the horizon: frontier-AI safety is becoming increasingly entangled with export controls and access to compute infrastructure.

Where product potential is emerging:
• audits of AI-stack dependency on a single cloud, API, region or compute supplier;
• switchable multi-provider model architectures with cost and quality degradation tested before a disruption happens.
