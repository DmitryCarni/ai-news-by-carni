AI News — October 1, 2026

Microsoft launched MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming and new MAI-Voice models, letting voice agents begin understanding speech before a person has finished talking.

California served OpenAI an investigative subpoena as part of a cybersecurity inquiry involving AI models.

ServiceNow launched Flow, an AI-native service desk that works inside everyday chat channels and turns repeated resolutions into automation.

DigitalOcean introduced Agent Droplets, bundling compute, memory, storage, inference and governed access to more than 16,000 tools into one subscription.

Armadin raised $255.5 million in Series B funding; Reuters reports a valuation above $2.5 billion for its autonomous AI security platform.

Anthropic launched mods for Claude Code, allowing TypeScript functions to rewrite prompts, intercept tool calls, handle permissions and alter the UI.

Main takeaway: agents are moving from demos into real operating environments, increasing the value of low latency, simpler infrastructure and strong action controls.

Product opportunities:

• policy and audit gateways for agent tools.
