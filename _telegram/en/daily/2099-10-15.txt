AI daily — October 15, 2099

🧪 TEST COPY of Oct 14 2026 archives, NOT current 2099 news. 🌦️ Microsoft Research introduced a method for improving weather forecasts two to six weeks ahead. AI corrects systematic errors in existing forecasting systems. Researchers reported better statistical performance across most evaluated precipitation, temperature and pressure targets. This does not mean exact local weather can be predicted weeks ahead without uncertainty.

🛡️ Anthropic identified a weakness in Claude's defenses against coordinated misuse. The model rejected direct harmful political requests but sometimes failed to recognize a malicious campaign divided into seemingly harmless tasks. Anthropic strengthened detection mechanisms. The key challenge is evaluating complete sequences of actions, not only individual prompts.

🏢 OpenAI analyzed enterprise adoption of AI agents in Europe. Codex and ChatGPT Work accounted for more than half of output tokens in the examined sample. Among smaller EU businesses, weekly active users of these tools increased 8.1 times since May. These figures measure product usage rather than independently verified financial savings.

🔬 NVIDIA and Siemens improved an engineering AI agent used in semiconductor design. In a controlled evaluation, its composite verification score increased from approximately 55% to 95%, while agent turns decreased by 66%. The underlying model stayed the same: improvements came from tool design, clearer instructions and rigorous output verification.

💰 UK-based Salience Labs raised $125 million for optical switching technology intended for AI data centers. Its products aim to improve communication between computing accelerators. Investors include AMD Ventures and Intel Capital. The financing is confirmed, but large-scale adoption and economic advantages still require operational validation.

📄 Conga introduced new ways to connect AI assistants with contracts, pricing and commercial quotations. Its MCP server exposes authorized enterprise information to compatible assistants, while AiMe now offers more than 90 tools and 19 skills. Consequential financial and legal actions still require appropriate verification and approval.

Key takeaway: AI is increasingly judged by measurable performance in real workflows. Output reliability, multistep safety, computing efficiency and access to enterprise information are becoming central to deployment. Governance and permission enforcement must develop alongside these capabilities.

Business opportunities:
• Evaluating and improving the reliability of enterprise AI agents.
• An AI assistant for reviewing contracts and commercial quotations.
• Correcting systematic errors in demand forecasts and business planning.
