🤖 AI Week in Review: agents get permission to act — and the market starts building brakes

September 1–6 was less about another model race and more about a change in deployment rules.

• GPT-6 Astra made a major jump in computer use and professional agent work — while becoming OpenAI's first broadly deployed model at the Critical cybersecurity capability level. Controlling agent actions is now architecture, not a safety task for later.

• Claude Fable 5.1 and Gemini 3.8 Flash pushed a second race forward: cost per completed task. Cache economics, trajectory length, retries and model routing increasingly matter more than the headline token price.

• Anthropic introduced Enterprise Frontier Safeguards: monitoring data for Claude can remain in the customer's own cloud under its keys and audit controls. That is a meaningful shift for banks and other regulated buyers.

• NVIDIA PAIR turns multiple local PCs into a small execution pool for parallel AI agents, making private and hybrid deployments more practical.

• The strongest finance signal: India is preparing UPI for small AI-agent payments without approval on every transaction, using predefined limits and delegated authority.
