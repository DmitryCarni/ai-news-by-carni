AI Week in Review

AI is getting cheaper, gaining more authority — and running into the control problem.

Five signals defined September 18–24:

• OpenAI and Anthropic both pushed down the cost of strong frontier models, making long-running agent work economical across more workflows.

• Autonomy is scaling with risk: cyber defense, banking and independent model evaluation increasingly require permissions, logging, verification and stop controls.

• China is building vertically across memory, models, accelerators and large clusters while regulators cool an overheated humanoid-robot market.

• Frontier models are moving deeper into real operating workflows at Airbnb, in cyber defense, capital projects and enterprise APIs.

• The agent interface is expanding into video, languages, corporate tools and physical AI.

Where product potential is emerging:

1. An independent control layer for enterprise agents: permissions, tool registry, action logs, limits, human approval and emergency stops.

2. Repricing expensive manual workflows around cheaper frontier models: code, documents, security, reconciliation and research.
